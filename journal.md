# 📖 Project Journal – 3D Printed Plastic Pot Stand

## Project Name: 3D Printed Plastic Pot Stand

**Project Type:** Hardware / CAD / 3D Printing

---

# Part 1 – Project Idea and Rough Sketch

At the beginning of the project, I wanted to make a simple and useful stand for keeping a flower pot above the ground. I wanted the stand to have a round shape and enough support so that the pot could sit properly on it. I decided to make the project as a hardware object instead of only making a design on the computer.

First, I looked at the size and shape of the flower pot that I wanted to use with the stand. I thought about the diameter, height and support required for the pot. After this, I made a rough sketch on paper. The sketch was not perfect because it was only used to understand the basic shape before starting the CAD work.

In the rough sketch, I marked the circular top area, the support legs and the side openings. I also thought about making the stand strong but not too heavy. I checked the sketch several times and changed some parts before starting the 3D modelling.

This first part helped me understand what I wanted to make and gave me a basic plan for the next stage.

### Activities Performed

- Selected the idea for the pot stand.
- Checked the approximate size of the flower pot.
- Made a rough hand sketch.
- Planned the circular shape and support structure.
- Thought about the size and stability of the stand.
- Made small changes to the rough design.

### Outcome

At the end of Part 1, I had a basic idea and rough sketch of the pot stand. I was ready to convert the sketch into a proper 3D model.

---

# Part 2 – 3D Modelling

After completing the rough sketch, I started making the proper 3D model on my computer using CAD software. I used the sketch as a reference and started creating the different parts of the stand.

First, I created the main circular shape of the stand. After that, I added the support structure and the openings on the sides. I tried to keep the design simple because I wanted it to be easy to print and strong enough to hold a flower pot.

I checked the model from different angles while designing it. I also checked the overall size and made changes where needed. Some parts of the first design did not look suitable, so I adjusted them before making the final version.

After completing the model, I checked the final 3D design again. I made sure the top area was suitable for the flower pot and that the bottom support was wide enough for stability.

My own 3D printer was not working at this time, so I could not print the model at home. Instead, I saved/exported the final CAD model and prepared the file for a local 3D printing shop.

### Activities Performed

- Started the CAD model using the rough sketch.
- Created the circular base.
- Added support legs.
- Added side openings.
- Checked the model from different views.
- Adjusted the dimensions.
- Finalized the 3D design.
- Exported the model for 3D printing.

### Challenge Faced

The main problem in this stage was that my own 3D printer was broken. Because of this, I could not directly print the model at home. I decided to use a local 3D printing shop instead of leaving the project only as a computer design.

### Outcome

At the end of Part 2, the complete 3D model was ready for printing. I had a final CAD file that could be given to a 3D printing service.

---

# Part 3 – Hardware Outcome

After completing the CAD model, I gave the 3D model file to a local 3D printing shop. I explained that I wanted the model printed as a plastic pot stand. The shop checked the file and accepted the printing order.

Since my own 3D printer was broken, using the local printing shop was the practical way to turn my computer design into a real hardware object. I submitted the model and waited for the printing to be completed.

After around **2 days**, I received the printed pot stand from the shop. I checked the printed object carefully to make sure the shape was correct and that there were no major printing problems.

I then compared the physical printed stand with my CAD model. The overall shape and support structure matched the design I had made on the computer. I also placed the flower pot on the stand to check whether it fitted properly.

The stand was able to hold the pot properly. I checked the stability by looking at the stand from different sides and making sure it was sitting correctly on the surface.

### Activities Performed

- Sent the CAD file to the local 3D printing shop.
- Placed the printing order.
- Waited for the printing process to finish.
- Received the printed hardware after around 2 days.
- Checked the printed shape and surface.
- Compared the printed object with the CAD model.
- Placed a flower pot on the stand.
- Checked the size and stability.

### Challenge Faced

The main challenge was not having a working 3D printer at home. The printing had to be done by a shop, so I had to wait for the order instead of printing immediately.

### Outcome

The CAD design was successfully converted into a real plastic hardware object. The printed pot stand matched the main design and was able to support the flower pot.
